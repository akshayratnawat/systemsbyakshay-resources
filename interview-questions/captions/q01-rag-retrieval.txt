AI Engineering Interview Question 1

Retriever returns the correct chunk, generator still hallucinates details. 15% hallucination rate overall. How do you debug it?

What this actually tests: Ability to isolate a failure to the generation stage instead of reflexively re-tuning retrieval.

How a strong answer breaks down:
→ Confirm retrieval precision/recall on a labeled eval set before touching the generator - if the right chunk is in context, the bug is downstream.
→ Check context position: a chunk buried in the middle of a long context gets ignored (lost-in-the-middle), verify chunk placement/ordering.
→ Check the prompt: is it explicitly telling the model to answer only from provided context, or letting it blend in prior knowledge.
→ Check chunk granularity: the right chunk retrieved but too broad, forcing the model to extract one detail from noise.

🚩 Red flag: Re-tuning the retriever/chunking without first verifying the correct chunk actually reached the model

🔁 Likely follow-up: If the hallucination is concentrated on numeric values specifically, what's your fix, and would a bigger model actually solve it or just hide it?
