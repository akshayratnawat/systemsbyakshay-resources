AI Engineering Interview Question 23

Your model performs great in staging, degrades within a week in production, then recovers on redeploy with the same weights. What's your hypothesis and how do you test it?

What this actually tests: Distinguishing a stateful/environmental degradation from a model-weights problem.

How a strong answer breaks down:
→ Name the clue explicitly - the same weights working again after redeploy rules out the model itself, this must be state accumulating in the running process over the week.
→ Hypothesize a memory/resource leak or cache growth that degrades performance over days and resets on redeploy.
→ Hypothesize a stateful cache or connection going stale over time (a connection pool degrading, a local cache not refreshing) that a fresh process wouldn't have.
→ Hypothesize numerical drift in a stateful component (a running average, adaptive threshold, or embedding cache) that quietly drifts and resets on redeploy.

🚩 Red flag: Treating this as a model/weights problem and considering retraining, despite the clue that rules it out

🔁 Likely follow-up: You isolate it to a feature cache with no TTL that starts serving stale features after about a week. What's the actual fix, and how do you make sure this class of bug doesn't happen again in a different cache somewhere else?
