AI Engineering Interview Question 16

Design a feature store for a fraud detection AI model - features must reflect the last 1, 5, and 30 minutes of activity per citizen ID, with retrieval under 10ms. How do you build this?

What this actually tests: Real-time feature engineering and low-latency serving architecture design.

How a strong answer breaks down:
→ Split into two paths: a streaming path maintaining rolling aggregates (1/5/30 min windows), and a serving path that just reads precomputed values - never compute aggregates at request time.
→ Use a stream processor (e.g. Flink/Kafka Streams) to maintain windowed aggregates per citizen ID incrementally as events arrive, updating a fast key-value store.
→ Serve from an in-memory or purpose-built low-latency KV store (e.g. Redis) keyed by citizen ID - disk-backed lookups won't consistently hit 10ms at scale.
→ Precompute and materialize the windows continuously - request-time aggregation over raw events cannot hit a 10ms bar.

🚩 Red flag: Computing windowed aggregates at request time by querying raw event logs, which cannot hit 10ms at this scale

🔁 Likely follow-up: The stream processor falls behind during a traffic spike and the 1-minute window is now 90 seconds stale. Does the fraud model still trust it, and what's your circuit breaker?
