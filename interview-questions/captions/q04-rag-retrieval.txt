AI Engineering Interview Question 4

Two documents contradict each other and your RAG system picks the wrong one 30% of the time. How do you fix retrieval ranking for this?

What this actually tests: Reasoning about ranking signals beyond pure semantic similarity, and about document trustworthiness.

How a strong answer breaks down:
→ Characterize the contradiction first: stale-vs-current (a versioning problem) or genuinely conflicting sources (a trust problem) - the fix differs.
→ If versioning: add recency/version metadata and boost or filter by it at retrieval time - this is the majority of real-world cases.
→ If trust: add a source-authority signal (canonical doc, owner, approval status) as a ranking feature, not pure cosine similarity.
→ Check whether the embedding model even distinguishes the two documents, or if they're near-duplicates it can't separate.

🚩 Red flag: Treating it as a chunking/embedding tuning problem when it's a data-governance problem

🔁 Likely follow-up: The two docs are both current and genuinely contradict each other, no stale version to prefer. What does the product actually do at answer time?
