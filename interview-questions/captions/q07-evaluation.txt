AI Engineering Interview Question 7

You want continuous evaluation measuring hallucination rate, refusal rate, Arabic quality, and more - how do you build that pipeline?

What this actually tests: Pipeline/systems design thinking for evaluation infrastructure, not just listing metrics.

How a strong answer breaks down:
→ Start with a versioned, curated eval set per dimension (hallucination, refusal, per-language quality), each with clear labeling criteria.
→ Decide a judge strategy per dimension: rule-based for refusal detection, LLM-as-judge for hallucination/quality, human spot-checks for calibration.
→ Run the eval suite automatically on every model/prompt change (CI-style gate), not just on a schedule.
→ Also run a scheduled slice against live production traffic samples, since offline sets drift from real usage.

🚩 Red flag: One giant static eval set with no versioning or refresh plan

🔁 Likely follow-up: Your LLM judge and human labelers agree 90% of the time. Is that good enough to trust the judge as the release gate, and what would change your answer?
